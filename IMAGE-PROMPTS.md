# Image generation prompts
Built-in image generation tool, three separate calls. All are generated stand-ins, including simulated human entries. Palette and brush width are approximations, not guaranteed pixel constraints.

Common prompt: landscape 2:1 pure white browser canvas. A pigeon being fired from its office job. A non-artist drawing with a trackpad in 15–25 seconds. Only 10–18 rough black #252620 and orange #fe653b strokes, uniform round pen, awkward proportions, gaps and overshoots. Mostly empty white space. No shape tools, typeset text, shading, texture, polished cartoon or UI.

1. pigeon-bye.png: small wonky pigeon, crooked legs, orange belongings box with handle and shakily handwritten “bye”.
2. pigeon-arrow.png: almost stick figure, oversized lopsided head, poorly joined body, tiny orange tie and crooked exit arrow. No words.
3. pigeon-desk.png: badly closed square-ish head, disconnected arcs for body, wing slash and messy legs. Orange X overhead and crude L-shaped desk. No words.

## Prompt Gone Wrong asset
`dist/assets/mystery-bowl.png` generated with the built-in image generation tool: photorealistic orange Thai prawn soup with three miniature cheerful Bengal tigers sitting in the bowl like a hot tub, giant prawns, lemongrass and coriander, warm restaurant lighting, landscape 3:2, no text, no distress or injury. Deliberately illustrates a misunderstanding of “tiger prawn soup”.
